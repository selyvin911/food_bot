# Privacy Policy

Nov 15, 2026

## Introduction

Menushka ("the App") is an iOS app for saving recipes, planning a weekly menu and building a shopping list. The App is operated by Maksim Iurchin ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the App, why we collect it, how long we keep it, with whom we share it, and what rights you have, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws where they apply.

By using the App you accept this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

The App works without registration. You do not give us your name, email address, phone number or password. Most of the data described below is tied to a random account identifier that the App creates on your device, not to you as a named person.

## 1. What data we collect and why

### 1.1 Categories of data

- **Anonymous account identifier.** On first launch the App signs you in anonymously and our backend creates a random user ID for you. It is stored in the secure storage (Keychain) of your device and sent with every request so that your recipes, menu and shopping list stay yours. It contains no name, email address or phone number.
- **Content you create in the App.** Your own recipes (title, steps, ingredients, servings, cooking time, tags and nutrition per serving), cover photos you pick from your photo library for those recipes, recipes you mark as favourites, your weekly menu (which recipe is planned for which day and meal), and the state of your shopping list (items you tick off, extra items you add, and store products you choose for an ingredient). We store this content on our backend so that it is available every time you open the App.
- **Links you submit for import.** When you import a recipe by link, we store the TikTok or Telegram URL you submitted, the status of the request, any error, and the resulting recipe copy. We also download the publicly available content of the linked post (description, automatic captions, post text, photo, and the author's or channel's public name) to produce the recipe. See section 2.
- **Settings.** Your chosen country for stores and prices (Belarus, Russia or Kazakhstan, taken from your device region by default) and your colour theme. They are stored on your device. The country is also sent to our analytics provider as a profile attribute (see below).
- **Device, technical and usage data.** Through the AppMetrica SDK we receive events about how the App is used (for example that a recipe was saved or an import was started), crash reports, performance and diagnostic data, the device model, operating system version, App version, language, IP address and an identifier generated by the SDK. This data is not linked to your account identifier or to you as a named person. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for tracking permission, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
- **Communications.** If you write to us, we keep your message, your contact details and our reply.
- **Purchase data.** The App currently has no paid features. If we add them, purchases will be processed by Apple, and we will update this Policy to describe the data involved.

### 1.2 Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access, or any health or body data. The App only receives a photo when you pick it yourself from your photo library as a recipe cover.

### 1.3 If you do not provide certain data

The anonymous identifier and the content you create are needed for the App to work; without them there is nothing to show. Importing by link is optional: if you do not submit links, no post content is downloaded or sent to an AI provider. Analytics and crash data are collected automatically to keep the App working and improve it.

## 2. Recipe import and AI processing

### 2.1 What happens when you submit a link

Our import service opens the public TikTok video or Telegram post you linked, downloads its description, automatic captions or post text and image, and sends that text together with our instructions to an AI model (Claude by Anthropic, accessed through OpenRouter) to extract the recipe: title, ingredients, amounts and steps. The result is saved as your personal copy of the recipe, visible only to you. Private or restricted posts and channels are not supported, and we do not log in to TikTok or Telegram on your behalf.

### 2.2 What the AI provider receives

The AI provider receives only the content of the linked post and our instructions. It does not receive your account identifier, your other recipes or any data about you. Under the published API terms of these providers, content sent through the API is not used to train their models.

### 2.3 Reuse and limits

A recipe extracted from a public post is derived from that post, not from you. If another user submits the same link, we may reuse the extracted recipe instead of processing the post again. To control costs, each account may import up to 5 recipes within any 3-day period; we count your requests to apply this limit.

## 3. How we use your data

We use the data described above to:

- provide the App: store and show your recipes, favourites, menu and shopping list, and keep them in sync across launches (performance of our contract with you);
- import recipes from links you submit and show you the result (performance of our contract with you);
- show products and prices of grocery chains for the country you selected (performance of our contract with you);
- apply usage limits, prevent abuse and keep the service secure (legitimate interests);
- understand how the App is used, find and fix crashes and errors, and improve features (legitimate interests; consent where local law requires it for analytics);
- answer your messages and support requests (legitimate interests and performance of our contract);
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms (legal obligation and legitimate interests).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles about you for marketing, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Recipe extraction by an AI model is an automated step, but it only processes the content of the public post you linked and never evaluates you.

## 4. Sharing of your personal data

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

### 4.1 Service providers

We rely on providers that process data on our behalf and on our instructions, under data processing agreements:

| Provider | What it does for the App | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database, anonymous authentication, file storage for recipe photos, realtime updates | Account identifier, your content, import requests |

Product names, prices and availability shown in the App come from the public websites of grocery chains in Belarus, Russia and Kazakhstan and are collected by us, not by your device. The App does not send any data about you to those chains, and we are not affiliated with them.

### 4.3 Other cases

- **Legal obligations.** We may disclose data if required by law, a court order or a public authority, or to enforce our Terms and protect the rights, property or safety of users, us or others.
- **Business transfers.** If we reorganise, merge or sell the App or our assets, your data may be transferred to the successor, who will remain bound by this Policy or an equivalent one.

## 5. International data transfers

Our providers operate in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States (Supabase, Railway, OpenRouter, Anthropic) and the Russian Federation (AppMetrica). Data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws of your country. Where the law requires it, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent measures for such transfers.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, unless the law requires or allows a longer period (for example, for accounting, disputes or enforcement).

- **Your content and account identifier:** for as long as your anonymous account exists. Deleting the App from your device does not delete your data on our servers, because the account can be restored on the same device. To delete the data, ask us (section 8). We may also delete accounts that have not been used for a long time.
- **Deleted recipes:** when you delete a recipe in the App, it is hidden from your catalogue and removed from future days of your menu. The record may remain in our database until your account data is deleted, so that past menu days keep their history.
- **Import requests:** stored with your account for the same period. Recipes extracted from public posts are not tied to you and may be kept after your data is deleted.
- **Analytics and crash data:** kept by our analytics provider for the period set in its terms, in a form that is not linked to your identity.
- **Communications:** while your request is open and for a reasonable time afterwards.

## 7. Security measures

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect data against loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure and access. In particular, all traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS), your session credentials are stored in your device's secure storage, and database access rules ensure that each account can read and change only its own data. Our editorial tools and import service use separate credentials that are never shipped inside the App.

No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your data, we will investigate it promptly and inform affected users and the competent authorities where the law requires it.

## 8. Your data protection rights

Depending on where you live, and in particular under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- **portability**: receive the content you created in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data (you can edit your recipes, menu and shopping list directly in the App);
- **erasure** of your data ("right to be forgotten");
- **restriction** of processing in certain circumstances;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests, including analytics;
- **withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that took place before;
- **lodge a complaint** with the data protection authority of your country. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

### 8.1 How to exercise your rights

Write to us at maxim.yurchin@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot find your data by your name or email address. We will explain how to find your account identifier in the App and ask you to send it; we may ask for additional information to make sure we are acting on the right account. We aim to respond within one month. For complex or numerous requests this period may be extended by up to two further months, and we will tell you if that happens.

### 8.2 Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything linked to it (recipes, photos, favourites, menu, shopping list and import requests), send us a request as described above. We will delete the data within one month, except what we must keep by law. Deleting the App from your device alone does not delete server-side data.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where local law sets a higher age for consent to data processing (for example, 16 in some countries of the European Union), that higher age applies. If you believe a child has used the App and we hold their data, contact us at maxim.yurchin@gmail.com and we will delete it.
